Conversation
Keep your keys and your sealed backup in separate places. Anyone who has both can read the chats; the sealed files alone are unreadable without the keys.

How this works. Witbitz stores your conversations as ciphertext it cannot read. Your keys live in your Space links and on your device. On the Confidential tier they reach our servers only sealed to a verified enclave; on the standard tier they are sent with each agent turn, so the stored record is unreadable from the store but the turn itself is handled in the clear. This page rebuilds the exact decryption the app uses (HKDF-SHA-256 → AES-256-GCM) with your browser’s built-in cryptography, with no network access. Save this file, your keys, and your sealed backup, and you can read your data forever — even if Witbitz is gone.
